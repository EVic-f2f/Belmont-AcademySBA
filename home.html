Belmout Academy

About Us

Jamaica's First Centre of Excellence opened its door in 2009 with over 200 students focusing on the sciences and services emphasising civic awareness, community service, critical thinking, pedagogy, technology and leadership.

Almost 10 years later the Academy's population is five times more than it was then and we are currently ranked in the top 30 schools island-wide.

Programs

At Belmont Academy, we are committed to providing programmes that inspire excellence, creativity, leadership, and lifelong success in every student.

Our programmes in Mathematics, English, Science, Business Education, Computing, Design and Technology, and the Performing Arts help students think critically and communicate confidently.

Admissions

Admissions at Belmont Academy are open to students who are ready to become part of a disciplined, achievement-oriented, and supportive learning community.

Applicants submit academic records and supporting documents, then are guided into programmes that fit their strengths and interests.
